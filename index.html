03 — Process

Small scope, documented evidence, clear handoff.

01

Define

Clarify the question, names, date range, geography, output format, and what counts as useful evidence.

02

Search

Work across Korean-language and international sources, prioritizing institutional and primary records where possible.

03

Verify

Cross-check names, dates, object data, accession numbers, and source relationships before synthesis.

04

Deliver

Return a concise brief, source table, bibliography, chronology, spreadsheet, or another agreed format.

Research between objects, records, and collections.

I study Art Studies at Hongik University and am developing a research practice around material culture, collections, archives, provenance, and cultural heritage. My working advantage is direct access to Korean-language sources and the ability to turn them into organized English-language research for international clients.
